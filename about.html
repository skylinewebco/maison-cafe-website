Our story

A decade of doing coffee, slowly

Maison began with a simple belief — that coffee should be an experience, not a transaction. What started as a tiny corner roastery has grown into a home for anyone who loves a well-made cup and an unhurried moment.

We still roast every batch ourselves, still greet regulars by name, and still believe the little details are what make the difference.

What we stand for

Our promise in every cup

Quality, uncompromised

We taste, score and roast until every bean earns its place. Good enough is never the goal.

People first

Fair partnerships with growers, fair wages for our team, and a warm welcome for every guest.

Kind to the planet

Compostable cups, zero-waste grounds and locally sourced bakery — small steps, taken seriously.

The craft

From green bean to golden crema

Every morning begins in our roastery. We cup each new arrival, dial in the roast, and rest the beans until they're ready. Only then do they reach your cup.
